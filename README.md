TrueNorth API provides business data for Canada and Australia through a single key, covering payroll, sales tax, GST, holidays, business days, postal codes, and business number validation. The service offers 1,000 free credits on registration, with pay‑per‑answer pricing and no credit‑card required. It includes endpoints for payroll calculation, tax classification, holiday calendars, postcode intelligence, ABN validation, and address checks, all accessible via a REST API.

**Areas:** Company · Business · Payroll · Tax · Canada · Australia

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for TrueNorth API: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
